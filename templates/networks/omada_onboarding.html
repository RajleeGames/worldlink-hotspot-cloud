Four simple steps

Works for businesses in different locations.

  1. Add your TP-Link Omada router, model and serial number from its label in Routers.
  2. Select WorldLink central Omada, enter the AP MAC from the label and the guest Wi-Fi name you want.
  3. Send for review. WorldLink creates or assigns a separate site for your business and provides a controller Inform URL.
  4. Enter the Inform URL in your Omada AP's controller settings (or ask our team for help). WorldLink adopts the AP, checks online status and then separately tests paid Wi-Fi.
Important: This onboarding form is not a hotspot operator login. WorldLink will never ask you to paste router, controller or EvPay passwords here. Each business has its own site. A new request cannot collect live customer payments or enable access by itself.
