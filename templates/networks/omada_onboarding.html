How setup works

Designed for customers in different locations.

  1. Register your Omada access point under Routers. Each business manages only its own devices.
  2. Enter the exact guest Wi-Fi SSID and AP MAC from the Omada Controller.
  3. If you already have secure private controller access, select private HTTPS/VPN and enter the controller origin and ID. Do not publish TCP port 8043 on the internet.
  4. If you are far away or behind carrier NAT, select Help me connect and describe your network. A technician will advise on a secure connection method.
  5. Click Request WorldLink review. The team can request more information before the router is connected.
Important: This onboarding form is not a hotspot operator login. WorldLink will never ask you to paste router, controller or EvPay passwords here. Existing Eaglelink live payment settings are not modified by this request.
